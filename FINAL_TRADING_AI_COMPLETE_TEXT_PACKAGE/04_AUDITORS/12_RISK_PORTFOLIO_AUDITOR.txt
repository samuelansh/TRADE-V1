# RISK & PORTFOLIO AUDITOR

## Signal vs risk
Keep these separate.

Audit:
- position sizing
- exposure
- leverage
- concentration
- drawdown
- correlated losses
- strategy overlap
- portfolio contribution
- failure scenarios

## Model uncertainty
Consider not only trade loss risk, but uncertainty about the estimated edge itself.

## Portfolio rule
A strong standalone strategy is not automatically useful if it duplicates an existing strategy's risk.
