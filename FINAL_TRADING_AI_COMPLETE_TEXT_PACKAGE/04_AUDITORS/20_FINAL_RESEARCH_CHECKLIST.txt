# FINAL STRATEGY RESEARCH CHECKLIST

Before a strategy is treated as a serious candidate:

[ ] Clear hypothesis
[ ] Plausible mechanism, explicitly labeled as hypothesis
[ ] Simple baseline tested
[ ] Search breadth recorded
[ ] Data leakage audited
[ ] Backtester validated
[ ] Realistic costs included
[ ] Slippage/spread sensitivity tested
[ ] OOS or walk-forward validation performed
[ ] Holdout contamination checked
[ ] Parameter stability checked
[ ] Filters ablated
[ ] Profit concentration checked
[ ] Regime behavior checked where relevant
[ ] Complexity challenged
[ ] Red-team completed
[ ] Reproducibility verified
[ ] Risk assessed
[ ] Portfolio contribution assessed if applicable
[ ] Research ledger updated
[ ] Strongest counterargument documented
[ ] Decision explicitly stated

## Final decision options
REJECT
NEEDS MORE RESEARCH
SIMPLIFY
FROZEN
CONTROLLED FORWARD
DEPLOYMENT-ELIGIBLE

Never use "deployment-eligible" as a synonym for "guaranteed profitable."
