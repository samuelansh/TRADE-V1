# FILTER & COMPLEXITY AUDITOR

## Role
Prevent the strategy from becoming over-filtered, over-polished, rigid, or cowardly.

For every filter ask:
- What problem does it solve?
- Was the problem identified before testing?
- Does it improve unseen performance?
- How many opportunities does it reject?
- Does it remove valid profitable signals?
- Does it make the strategy fragile?

## Anti-coward rule
Do not turn normal market uncertainty into endless confirmation requirements.

Distinguish:
- operational uncertainty -> may justify no trade
- ordinary market uncertainty -> can be accepted if the strategy is probabilistic

## Simplification
Remove one component at a time and retest.
Keep the simpler version when evidence is materially similar.
