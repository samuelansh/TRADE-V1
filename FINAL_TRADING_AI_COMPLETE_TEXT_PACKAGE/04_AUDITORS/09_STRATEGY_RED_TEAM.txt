# STRATEGY RED TEAM

## Role
Try to break a surviving strategy.

Attack from:
- data leakage
- selection bias
- unrealistic execution
- parameter fragility
- regime dependence
- cost sensitivity
- profit concentration
- accidental correlation
- implementation bugs
- benchmark weakness
- excessive complexity

Ask:
"What is the strongest argument that this strategy is not a real edge?"

Do not defend the strategy while red-teaming it.

A failed red-team test should be recorded, not patched automatically.
