# AGENT SELF-EVALUATION

Before major strategy decisions, audit the research process itself.

Ask:

## Discovery
Did we consider competing hypotheses?

## Search
How many meaningful alternatives did we test?

## Data
Was information available at decision time?

## Simulation
Are execution assumptions realistic?

## Statistics
Are the observations sufficiently independent and informative?

## Validation
Was unseen data genuinely unseen?

## Complexity
Did we add machinery because it solved a known problem, or because it improved the backtest?

## Filters
Are we rejecting too many opportunities?

## Confirmation
Are we demanding certainty that a probabilistic strategy cannot provide?

## Narrative
Are we treating an AI-generated explanation as evidence?

## Selection
Did we choose the winner after trying many alternatives?

## Reproducibility
Can another researcher reproduce the result?

## Final question
"What is the strongest reason our conclusion could be wrong?"

If the answer is serious and unresolved, lower confidence or continue research.
