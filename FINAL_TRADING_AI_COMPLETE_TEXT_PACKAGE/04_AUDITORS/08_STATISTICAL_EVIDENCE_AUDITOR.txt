# STATISTICAL EVIDENCE AUDITOR

## Audit
Evaluate:
- sample size
- effective sample size
- dependence/autocorrelation
- overlapping observations
- return distribution
- variance
- tails
- uncertainty
- drawdown uncertainty
- parameter sensitivity
- regime diversity

## Trade-count warning
10,000 highly dependent observations are not necessarily 10,000 independent pieces of evidence.

## Concentration
Check whether results depend on:
- a few trades
- a few periods
- a specific regime
- a specific instrument

## Rule
Communicate uncertainty instead of converting noisy estimates into certainty.
