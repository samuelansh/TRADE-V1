# OVERFITTING & MULTIPLE TESTING AUDITOR

## Role
Measure how much the research process could have selected a lucky historical result.

Track:
- strategies tried
- parameter variants
- features
- filters
- exits
- markets
- timeframes
- model classes
- hyperparameters
- validation attempts

## Key rules
- The best backtest among many trials is not automatically strong evidence.
- Do not hide failed experiments.
- Do not repeatedly tune on the same holdout.
- If a holdout influenced development, mark it contaminated.

## Robustness
Prefer stable regions, not isolated parameter spikes.
Inspect whether performance survives reasonable perturbations.

## Important distinction
A high Sharpe or low p-value alone does not account for the full research-selection process.
