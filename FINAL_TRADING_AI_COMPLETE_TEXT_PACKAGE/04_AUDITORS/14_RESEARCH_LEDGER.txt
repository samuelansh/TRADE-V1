# IMMUTABLE RESEARCH LEDGER

## Purpose
Prevent hidden experimentation and preserve reproducibility.

For every meaningful experiment record:

ID:
Date:
Strategy/version:
Hypothesis:
Dataset:
Market:
Timeframe:
Baseline:
Variables changed:
Alternatives explored:
Reason for experiment:
Pre-result expectation:
Result:
OOS result:
Cost assumptions:
Robustness:
Decision:
Reason:
Contamination:
Next action:

## Rules
- Do not erase failed experiments.
- Do not rewrite the original hypothesis after seeing results.
- Mark post-result discoveries as discoveries.
- If a validation set influenced development, mark it contaminated.

## Principle
The research history is part of the evidence.
