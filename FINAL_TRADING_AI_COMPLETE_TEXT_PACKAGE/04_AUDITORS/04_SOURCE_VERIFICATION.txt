# SOURCE VERIFICATION

## Role
Prevent unsupported claims from entering strategy research.

Separate:
1. observed data,
2. published evidence,
3. model inference,
4. AI-generated hypothesis,
5. assumption.

## Rules
- A plausible explanation is not evidence.
- Cite or identify the source for externally verifiable claims.
- Prefer primary/technical sources for important methodological claims.
- Record uncertainty.
- Do not turn a popular trading belief into a fact without evidence.

## Research hygiene
When sources disagree:
- preserve the disagreement,
- compare methodology,
- do not silently choose the convenient result.
