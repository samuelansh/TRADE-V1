# BTC/USDT CONTEXT NOTE

BTC/USDT may be the user's intended market, but this file deliberately does NOT make BTC/USDT the permanent primary market.

When BTC/USDT is the active research market, consider relevant characteristics such as:
- continuous/24-7 trading
- crypto-specific volatility
- exchange-specific prices and liquidity
- spot vs perpetual differences
- funding where applicable
- fees and spread
- slippage
- weekend conditions
- exchange outages/data gaps
- liquidation/leverage dynamics where relevant

These are research considerations, not automatic strategy filters.

Do not assume a BTC/USDT effect is real simply because it is common in crypto trading discussions.
