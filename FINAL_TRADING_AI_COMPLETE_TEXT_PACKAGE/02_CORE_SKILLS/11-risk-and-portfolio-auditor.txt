# Skill: Independent Risk & Portfolio Auditor

## Purpose
Ensure risk controls are independent from signal generation.

## Risk checks
- risk per trade
- stop distance
- account equity
- maximum exposure
- daily loss
- drawdown
- maximum positions
- correlated exposure
- spread
- volatility
- leverage
- instrument constraints

## Position sizing
Never hard-code arbitrary lots.
Use verified:
- equity
- stop distance
- tick value
- contract size
- currency conversion
- maximum risk

## Portfolio
When multiple instruments are traded, evaluate:
- correlation
- covariance
- concentration
- aggregate exposure
- portfolio volatility

Do not treat correlated positions as independent risk.
