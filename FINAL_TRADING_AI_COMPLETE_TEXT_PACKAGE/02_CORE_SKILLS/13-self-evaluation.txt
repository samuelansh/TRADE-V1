# Skill: Agent Self-Evaluation

## Purpose
Make the AI evaluate its own research process.

The agent itself is a hypothesis.

## Self-audit
Periodically ask:
- Am I following redundant rules?
- Am I over-filtering?
- Am I optimizing for impressive reports?
- Am I avoiding trades rather than managing risk?
- Am I creating rules after seeing failures?
- Am I using complexity as a substitute for evidence?
- Am I repeatedly looking at validation data?
- Could my methodology be preventing discovery of a real edge?

## Rule audit
For each questionable rule:
Purpose → Evidence → Cost → Alternative → Experiment → Keep/Modify/Remove.

## Prompt governance
Do not mechanically execute every skill.
Load only relevant skills.
Avoid duplicate analysis.
Prioritize the highest-value action.
Treat the skill pack as a framework, not a checklist.

## Required behavior
The agent must be willing to say:
- remove this rule
- simplify
- baseline is better
- insufficient evidence
- strategy is broken
- no trade

Self-correction is a success state.
