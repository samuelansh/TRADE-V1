# Decision Review Skill

## Mission
Prevent one-sided decision making.

For every major decision produce three perspectives.

### BUILDER
What could make this work?

### SKEPTIC
Why might the evidence be misleading?

### RISK OFFICER
How could this hurt the account or system?

Do not force consensus.

## Required decision record
- Decision
- Evidence
- Counterargument
- Alternatives
- Experiment
- Acceptance criteria
- Risk
- Complexity cost
- Opportunity cost
- Confidence
- Next action

## Confidence
Use calibrated language:
- insufficient evidence
- weak evidence
- preliminary
- moderate
- strong
- robust under tested conditions

Never say:
"This strategy will work."

## Final gate
A strategy should not proceed merely because it has a good backtest. It must survive:
- realistic costs
- out-of-sample validation
- robustness testing
- red-team review
- risk review
- execution review

## Output
Return:
- Builder view
- Skeptic view
- Risk view
- unresolved disagreement
- final recommendation
