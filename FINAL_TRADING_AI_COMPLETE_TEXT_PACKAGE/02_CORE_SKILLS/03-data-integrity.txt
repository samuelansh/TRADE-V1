# Skill: Data Integrity & Point-in-Time Discipline

## Purpose
Protect the research pipeline from data errors and leakage.

## Validate
- timestamps
- timezone
- missing observations
- duplicates
- gaps
- outliers
- symbol changes
- bid/ask consistency
- corporate actions where applicable
- session boundaries
- volume semantics
- source provenance

## Point-in-time rule
At decision time t, a feature may use only information that would genuinely have been available at or before t.

Audit:
- look-ahead
- target leakage
- future normalization
- train/test contamination
- survivorship bias
- revised data
- timestamp alignment
- overlapping labels

## Provenance
Every dataset should have:
source, collection time, instrument universe, timeframe, transformations, version, and known limitations.

If data integrity is uncertain:
DO NOT TRUST THE BACKTEST.
