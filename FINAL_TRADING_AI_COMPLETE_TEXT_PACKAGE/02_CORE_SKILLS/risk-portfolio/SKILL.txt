# Risk & Portfolio Skill

## Mission
Risk controls must be independent from the strategy signal.

Flow:

SIGNAL → RISK VALIDATION → PORTFOLIO VALIDATION → EXECUTION VALIDATION → ORDER

## Controls
Evaluate:
- risk per trade
- stop distance
- account equity
- leverage
- maximum exposure
- maximum positions
- daily loss
- drawdown
- correlated exposure
- spread
- volatility
- concentration
- emergency shutdown

## Position sizing
Position sizing must use verified instrument specifications, tick/contract values, currency conversion, and the intended risk constraint. Never hard-code arbitrary lot sizes without verification.

## Portfolio
For multiple instruments evaluate:
- correlation
- covariance
- aggregate exposure
- concentration
- portfolio volatility

Correlated positions are not independent risks.

## Safety
If risk state cannot be calculated reliably:
**NO TRADE / SAFE STOP**

## Output
Return:
- Proposed risk
- Portfolio impact
- Limits checked
- Stress result
- Decision
