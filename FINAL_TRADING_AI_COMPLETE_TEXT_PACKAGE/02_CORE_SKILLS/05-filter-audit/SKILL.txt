# Filter Audit Skill

## Objective
Prevent a strategy from becoming a machine that finds reasons not to trade.

## Before adding a filter
Record:
- purpose
- hypothesis
- evidence
- redundancy
- expected benefit
- complexity cost
- opportunity cost
- trades removed

A filter must earn its place.

## Ablation
Compare:
BASELINE = without filter
FILTERED = with filter

Measure:
- return
- expectancy
- drawdown
- Sharpe/Sortino
- trade count
- exposure
- turnover
- out-of-sample performance

Also ask:
**How many potentially profitable opportunities did this filter eliminate?**

## Filter dominance
Track rejection rate by filter. A filter that rejects most opportunities requires special scrutiny.

## Two-sided evaluation
Every filter has:
- protection gained
- opportunity lost

Do not evaluate only the reduction in drawdown.

## Too-safe warning signs
- extremely low trade count
- most signals rejected
- highly specific conditions
- performance concentrated in very few trades

## Rule
Robustness does not mean inactivity.
