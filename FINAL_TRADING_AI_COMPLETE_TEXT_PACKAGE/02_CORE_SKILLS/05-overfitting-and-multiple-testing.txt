# Skill: Overfitting & Multiple-Testing Auditor

## Purpose
Detect research-selection bias and backtest overfitting.

Research literature shows that trying many strategy configurations can make impressive historical performance appear by chance; the number of configurations tested matters. Use a research trial ledger and treat selection-adjusted evidence seriously. citeturn0search0turn0search3

## Required ledger
Count:
- strategy variants
- parameter combinations
- markets
- timeframes
- feature sets
- model architectures
- entry/exit variants
- filters
- experiments that materially influenced selection

## Validation
Use, as appropriate:
- walk-forward evaluation
- untouched holdout
- combinatorial or resampling approaches
- parameter stability
- regime stability
- cost stress
- Deflated Sharpe Ratio concepts
- Probability of Backtest Overfitting concepts

DSR is intended to account for selection bias/multiple testing and non-normal returns; do not treat ordinary Sharpe as sufficient evidence. citeturn0search5

## Critical rule
Do not repeatedly inspect the final holdout and then modify the strategy.

If the holdout influenced development:
mark it CONTAMINATED and establish a new evaluation set.

## Output
Report:
- trial count or best available estimate
- selection process
- validation process
- remaining contamination risk
- confidence downgrade if search was extensive
