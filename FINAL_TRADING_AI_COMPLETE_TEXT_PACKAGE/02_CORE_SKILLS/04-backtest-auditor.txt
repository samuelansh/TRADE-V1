# Skill: Backtest Auditor

## Purpose
Determine whether a backtest is a credible simulation rather than a performance illustration.

## Audit
- signal timing
- order timing
- bid/ask
- spread
- commission
- slippage
- latency where material
- order type
- partial fills where relevant
- trading hours
- position sizing
- stop/TP behavior
- portfolio constraints
- instrument specifications

## Required comparisons
Run:
1. idealized assumptions
2. realistic assumptions
3. stressed assumptions

If the edge disappears under reasonable costs or execution uncertainty, downgrade or reject it.

## Never assume
Signal price equals fill price.
Submitted order equals executed order.
Historical spread equals future spread.
Backtest availability equals live availability.

## Audit output
Verdict:
PASS / CONDITIONAL / FAIL

List the assumptions most capable of changing the conclusion.
