# Trade Journal Skill

## Executed trade fields
Record:
- timestamp
- instrument
- timeframe
- strategy/version
- regime
- direction
- entry
- exit
- SL
- TP
- size
- spread
- risk
- signal reason
- execution result
- P/L
- MFE
- MAE
- exit reason

## Rejected signal fields
Record:
- timestamp
- instrument
- signal
- strategy/version
- rejection reason
- risk state
- market state

## Principle
A rejected trade is information too.

Do not only analyze trades that were executed.

## Integrity
Journal records should be append-only where practical and linked to strategy/data/experiment versions.
