# Skill: Continuous Improvement Without Post-Hoc Overfitting

## Purpose
Learn from results without turning every failure into a new filter.

## After a result
1. Observe.
2. Classify the result.
3. Decide whether it is expected variance or evidence of a structural problem.
4. Formulate a general hypothesis.
5. Log it before testing.
6. Define acceptance criteria.
7. Test against baseline and unseen data.
8. Keep, modify, or reject.

## Never do
"That historical trade was bad, so add a rule that would have prevented it."

Instead ask:
"What general mechanism could explain this failure?"

## Strategy DNA
Maintain the original hypothesis.
Track drift.
If the strategy becomes fundamentally different, create a new version.

## Stopping
When evidence is sufficient:
freeze the strategy and validate independently.

Do not optimize forever.
