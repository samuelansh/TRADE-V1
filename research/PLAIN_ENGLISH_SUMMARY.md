# What we actually did — in plain English

## The idea you gave me
You gave me a rulebook for building a trading AI. The rulebook's #1 rule,
in simple words: **"Don't trust a trading idea until you've tried very hard
to prove it wrong."** Most people skip this. That's why most people lose.

## What we did
1. We collected 15+ years of real Bitcoin, Ethereum and Litecoin price data
   and checked it for errors (found and threw out one broken dataset).
2. We built a "flight simulator" for trading — it replays history and tests
   an idea as if we had traded it back then, INCLUDING the fees brokers
   charge. Fees are the silent killer of trading ideas.
3. We tested 3 classic strategy ideas, the fair way:
   - **Idea 1: "Ride the trend"** (buy when price has been rising).
     Looked amazing on old data. Failed on recent data (2023-2026).
   - **Idea 2: "Buy the dip"** (buy after sharp drops, sell quickly).
     Fees ate it alive. Failed immediately.
   - **Idea 3: "Hold whichever coin is hottest"** (rotate between coins).
     Looked incredible on 2016-2018 data. Fell apart on 2019-2022 data.

## The result
**None of the three ideas is good enough to trade with, not even with fake
demo money.** That is our honest answer.

## Why this is a WIN, not a loss
Imagine a friend who backtests Idea 1, sees the amazing old results, puts
$1,000 in... and then lives through 2023-2026 where it does worse than just
holding Bitcoin. Our system caught that for free, using math instead of
your money. The rulebook you gave me calls this a SUCCESS, and it's right.

The professional word for what we produced is a "null result" — we proved
these simple ideas don't work anymore under real-world costs. Knowing what
DOESN'T work is real knowledge. Hedge funds pay millions to learn this.

## The uncomfortable truth (mentor mode)
- Roughly 70-90% of ordinary people who trade actively LOSE money.
- The simple strategies you can find on YouTube are exactly the ones we
  just tested. They're famous BECAUSE they used to work. Everyone knows
  them now, so the easy profit is gone.
- Anyone who promises you a bot that "makes money every day" is either
  fooling themselves or fooling you.

## What you actually gained (worth more than money right now)
- A real research pipeline: data checking, honest backtesting, risk
  controls, paper trading. This is how actual quant funds work.
- Proof that you can run a scientific process: make a claim, freeze the
  test, accept the answer even when it says "no".
- These skills (Python, statistics, skepticism) are the same ones that get
  people hired into finance and tech jobs later.

## What's next — realistic options
1. **Let the paper-trading robot keep running.** It trades with imaginary
   money using live prices. Zero risk. We check on it every few weeks like
   a science experiment.
2. **Learn mode.** Use this project to learn Python and statistics — you
   now own a genuinely professional-grade codebase to poke at.
3. **Feed it better data someday.** Minute-by-minute price data opens new
   kinds of ideas we haven't tested. No rush.
4. **What NOT to do:** put real money into any of this. Not because you're
   15 — because the math says no, for anyone.
